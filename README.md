- 🎞 Family Videos
- 🎙 Voice Recordings
- ✉ Letters
- 📖 Diaries
- 🪖 Military Records
- 🏅 Certificates

AI reconstructs a complete life story and produces a beautiful documentary.

# Why MemoryClip?

Every family owns priceless memories.

Unfortunately,

old photos fade.

letters disappear.

stories are forgotten.

videos become unreadable.

When a person passes away,

their memories slowly disappear.

MemoryClip exists to preserve those memories forever.

Not as a photo album.

Not as a document.

But as a film.

# Core Features

## 🪦 Gravestone Intelligence

Upload one gravestone photo.

MemoryClip automatically extracts:

- Name
- Birth date
- Death date
- Religion
- Memorial text
- Family members
- Cemetery
- GPS
- OCR

---

## 📖 Biography Engine

Transform fragmented memories into a coherent biography.

AI organizes:

- Childhood
- Education
- Career
- Marriage
- Children
- Military Service
- Retirement
- Legacy

---

## 🕰 Timeline Builder

Automatically generate a complete timeline.

```
1932

↓

School

↓

Army

↓

Marriage

↓

Children

↓

Retirement

↓

Legacy
```

Every timeline node becomes a documentary chapter.

---

## 👨 Family Tree

Generate relationships automatically.

```
Grandfather

↓

Father

↓

Children

↓

Grandchildren
```

Each person can own an independent documentary.

---

## 🌍 Historical Context

MemoryClip understands history.

Example:

```
Vietnam War

↓

What happened?

↓

How did it affect him?

↓

Historical footage

↓

Narration
```

Every personal story is connected with world history.

---

## 🖼 AI Photo Restoration

Restore damaged memories.

Supported:

- Scratch removal
- Denoising
- Face restoration
- Super Resolution
- Colorization
- HDR Enhancement

---

## 🎬 AI Documentary Generation

Generate documentaries instead of slideshows.

Includes:

- Chapters
- Narration
- Cinematic transitions
- Dynamic camera movement
- AI generated scenes
- Historical footage
- Maps
- Timeline animation

---

## 🎙 AI Narration

Natural multilingual narration.

Supported:

- English
- Chinese
- Japanese
- Korean
- Spanish
- French
- German

Multiple narrator styles:

- Documentary
- Emotional
- Historical
- Family
- Memorial

---

## 🎵 Music Engine

Automatically selects background music.

Examples:

- Piano
- Orchestra
- Ambient
- Memorial
- Hope
- Epic

---

## 🎥 Image-to-Video

Old photographs become alive.

```
Photo

↓

Animation

↓

Talking Portrait

↓

Image-to-Video

↓

Scene
```

The core AI system.

Responsible for:

- OCR
- Entity Recognition
- Biography Extraction
- Timeline Reconstruction
- Relationship Analysis
