# Workflow

> MemoryClip Workflow
>
> From fragmented memories to a cinematic documentary.

---

# Introduction

The MemoryClip workflow is intentionally different from traditional AI video products.

Traditional workflow

```
Prompt

↓

Generate

↓

Video
```

MemoryClip workflow

```
Memories

↓

Knowledge

↓

Biography

↓

Timeline

↓

Story

↓

Scenes

↓

Documentary
```

The workflow is evidence-driven rather than prompt-driven.

---

# Core Philosophy

The workflow follows one simple rule.

> Never generate before understanding.

Every AI generation stage must consume structured knowledge instead of raw user input.

          ▼          ▼          ▼          ▼
 Biography Agent Timeline Agent Historian Agent Family Agent

# Design Philosophy

The workflow is deliberately layered.

Raw memories become verified facts.

Verified facts become structured knowledge.

Structured knowledge becomes stories.

Stories become documentaries.

The documentary is the final expression of the workflow—not its foundation.

MemoryClip therefore preserves not only videos, but the knowledge required to recreate them for future generations, regardless of how AI technology evolves.
