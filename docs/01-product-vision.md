# Product Vision

> MemoryClip
>
> AI Documentary Studio for Human Memories

---

# Vision

Every person has a story worth remembering.

Most stories disappear.

Old photographs fade.

Letters become unreadable.

Family stories are forgotten.

Voice recordings are lost.

Eventually, only a gravestone remains.

MemoryClip exists to preserve human memories forever.

Not as files.

Not as folders.

Not as photo albums.

But as cinematic documentaries.

---

# Mission

Enable every family to create a beautiful documentary about the people they love.

MemoryClip should make documentary creation as simple as uploading memories.

Upload.

Remember.

Preserve.

Share.

---

# Philosophy

Traditional AI video products generate content.

MemoryClip reconstructs history.

Traditional products answer:

"How do I create a video?"

MemoryClip answers:

"Who was this person?"

This difference changes everything.

MemoryClip is not a video editor.

It is an AI memory reconstruction system.

# Design Principles

## Human First

Technology should disappear.

The person remains the center.

---

## Memory Before Media

Media files are only evidence.

The story is more important.

---

## Truth Before Beauty

The system should never fabricate historical facts.

When uncertain:

- Mark assumptions
- Request confirmation
- Cite available evidence

Historical integrity is essential.

---

## Family Collaboration

A biography is rarely owned by one person.

Multiple family members should be able to contribute:

- Photos
- Stories
- Corrections
- Voice recordings
- Documents

The documentary evolves collaboratively.

---

## Living Archive

The documentary is never final.

New memories can always be added.

Every generation can continue expanding the archive.

---
