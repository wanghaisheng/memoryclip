# Design Principles

> MemoryClip Design System
>
> Designing AI that preserves human memories.

---

# Introduction

MemoryClip is not another AI video generator.

It is an AI documentary system.

Every design decision should answer one question:

> Does this help preserve human memories more truthfully?

If the answer is no,
the feature should not exist.

---

# Principle 01
# Memory First

Memory is the primary asset.

Everything else exists to serve memory.

Never optimize for visual effects before preserving memories.

Priority:

Memory

↓

Story

↓

Emotion

↓

Video

↓

Effects

---

# Principle 02
# Truth Before Beauty

Documentaries should never invent history.

AI should distinguish between:

Known

Estimated

Unknown

Example

✓ Birth Date

Known

✓ Marriage Year

Estimated

✗ Military Rank

Unknown

Unknown information should remain unknown.

Never hallucinate.

---

# Principle 03
# Evidence Graph

Every statement should be traceable.

Example

John was born in 1932.

↓

Evidence

↓

Gravestone

↓

OCR

↓

Confidence

99%

Every biography paragraph should have evidence.

---

# Principle 04
# Human In The Loop

AI proposes.

Humans approve.

MemoryClip is collaborative.

Family members can

Approve

Reject

Edit

Comment

Upload evidence

No AI output should become permanent automatically.

---

# Principle 05
# Memories Are Living

A documentary is never finished.

Users can always add

New photos

New recordings

New documents

New memories

The documentary updates continuously.

---

# Principle 06
# Biography Before Script

Never generate documentary narration directly.

Pipeline

Raw Assets

↓

Biography

↓

Timeline

↓

Story

↓

Scenes

↓

Narration

This separation improves quality dramatically.

---

# Principle 07
# Timeline Is Canon

Timeline becomes the source of truth.

Every documentary scene references one timeline event.

Example

1968

↓

Vietnam

↓

Army

↓

Photos

↓

Narration

↓

Video

Everything originates from the timeline.

---

# Principle 08
# Documentary Language

MemoryClip speaks like documentaries.

Instead of

"He was born."

Use

"In the winter of 1932,
a child entered the world."

Language should feel cinematic,
not robotic.

---

# Principle 09
# Historical Context

Every person belongs to history.

MemoryClip should connect

Personal Event

↓

Historical Event

↓

World Context

Example

Born

1942

↓

World War II

↓

Historical background

↓

Personal story

History enriches biographies.

---

# Principle 10
# Asset Canon

Assets never disappear.

Every uploaded file becomes part of a permanent archive.

Examples

Photo

Letter

Certificate

Passport

Voice

Diary

Video

Gravestone

No duplication.

No data loss.

Everything receives a unique Asset ID.

# Principle 11
# Everything Is Connected

Memory is a graph.

People

↓

Relationships

↓

Places

↓

Events

↓

Assets

↓

Stories

↓

Documentaries

The graph continuously grows.

---

# Principle 12
# AI Transparency

Users should always know

What AI generated

What users uploaded

What historical records confirmed

Example

Generated Biography

✓

Verified by Family

✓

Historical Source

Wikipedia

Confidence

94%

Transparency builds trust.

---

# Principle 13
# Explainability

Every AI decision should answer

Why?

Example

Why was this photo selected?

↓

Because

Timeline

1968

↓

Vietnam

↓

Highest similarity score

Explainability is mandatory.

---

# Principle 14
# Non-Destructive Editing

Original memories are sacred.

Never overwrite

Original Photos

Original Audio

Original Videos

Restoration creates new versions.

Original assets remain untouched forever.

---

# Principle 15
# Emotional Authenticity

The goal is emotion.

Not visual complexity.

Simple photographs
can be more powerful than
AI-generated scenes.

Never replace real memories unnecessarily.

---

# Principle 16
# Human Face Consistency

A person should always look like themselves.

Across

Portraits

Videos

Restoration

Animation

Generated images

Identity consistency is critical.

---

# Principle 17
# Time Consistency

A person's appearance changes over time.

Young

↓

Adult

↓

Middle Age

↓

Old Age

AI should understand aging naturally.

---

# Principle 18
# Documentary Rhythm

Every documentary follows rhythm.

Introduction

↓

Background

↓

Conflict

↓

Transformation

↓

Legacy

The rhythm should emerge naturally from life events.

---

# Principle 19
# Accessibility

Everyone should use MemoryClip.

Requirements

Simple UI

Large fonts

Voice support

Keyboard support

Screen reader support

Offline mode

Families include elderly users.

Design accordingly.

---

# Principle 20
# AI Assists,
Humans Remember

MemoryClip is not replacing memories.

It helps humans remember better.

Technology should disappear.

Stories remain.

---

# Design Checklist

Before shipping any feature,
ask:

□ Does this preserve memories?

□ Does it improve truthfulness?

□ Does it increase emotional impact?

□ Can families understand it?

□ Does it respect history?

□ Is AI transparent?

□ Can evidence be traced?

□ Is the original asset preserved?

□ Is collaboration supported?

□ Does it improve the documentary?

If any answer is "No",

the feature needs redesign.

---

# Final Principle

People are not datasets.

They are lives.

Every document.

Every photograph.

Every voice recording.

Every gravestone.

Represents someone who existed.

MemoryClip should treat every memory with dignity.

That principle outweighs every algorithm.
